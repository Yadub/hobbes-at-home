[Your app's name] is a personal app that I, [your name], use to see my own WHOOP data in Hobbes at Home,
a dashboard that runs on my own Mac. It is not offered to anyone else.

## What it reads

With my permission, given on WHOOP's own sign-in page, it reads my WHOOP profile (name and email), body
measurements, cycles (strain and energy), recovery, sleep and workouts.

## Where the data goes

- It's stored only on my own Mac, in a folder that only my macOS account can read. The sign-in tokens are
  kept in the Mac's Keychain.
- It's never sold, shared or sent to anyone else, except as below.
- If I turn on "Share With the AI" in Hobbes at Home, a summary of it is sent to the AI service I chose
  (Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT), under my own account, to answer my own questions. This is off
  unless I turn it on.

## Keeping and deleting it

I can disconnect at any time in Hobbes at Home or in the WHOOP app, and delete the data by deleting the
app's folder on my Mac (`~/.hobbes`).
